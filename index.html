Deal Video Assistant

A personal tool for preparing and sharing short product videos.

Deal Video Assistant is a small personal application operated by YOUR_NAME. It helps the owner create short vertical videos about products and recommendations, and upload them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account, where the owner reviews, edits and publishes them manually.

What it does

  • Prepares scripts, captions and short videos from product information supplied by the owner.
  • Uses TikTok Login Kit so the owner can authorize the app to access their own TikTok account.
  • Uses the TikTok Content Posting API (video.upload scope) to send finished videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect personal data from visitors to this website.
  • It does not publish anything automatically without the owner's review in the TikTok app.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Disclosure

Videos prepared with this tool may contain affiliate links or promoted products. Such content is labeled as sponsored (#publi / commercial content disclosure) when published.
